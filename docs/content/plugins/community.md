A list of community created plugins and integrations for the Nacara platform.

## Stylesheets

- [TailwindCss](https://github.com/shayanhabibi/Partas.Nacara.Plugins) - [TailwindCss](https://tailwindcss.com/) for Nacara; configurable
- [DaisyUI](https://github.com/shayanhabibi/Partas.Nacara.Plugins) - [DaisyUI](https://daisyui.com/) for Nacara; configurable, routes through the [TailwindCss](https://github.com/shayanhabibi/Partas.Nacara.Plugins) plugin.
